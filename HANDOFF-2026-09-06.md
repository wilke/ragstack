## 5. The chunking study — where the science actually stands

### Settled

- **Overlap earns nothing and can be cut.** Powered null on both legs, |Δ recall@100| ≤ 0.0033,
  at up to 1.32× the vectors. This is the largest actionable result Phase 0 produced, and it
  rests on bounded-interval evidence from Legs A and B. **Stage 0's overlap contrast does not
  bear on it** in either direction — see the trap in §6.
- **BEIR corpora cannot test large chunks.** `trec-covid`: 171,332 documents, median 378 tokens,
  **max 925**. Replaced by TREC CDS 2014–16 (90 human-judged topics, PMC OA JATS, median 4,097
  body tokens, 98.5% fetchable). Already in `MEMORY.md`.
- **The reranker truncates at 4,096 tokens per query–chunk pair**, so 2048-token chunks are
  interpretable. Two independent signatures: plateau at 0.7808, collapse to 0.0025.
- **Every metric before the passage-level work was a *document* metric.** On a one-document
  corpus, where document hit@1 is 1.000 by arithmetic, the top chunk lands in the answering
  section only 55–65% of the time. The load-bearing number is passage hit@1 against its chance
  rate — lift over random is 3.49× / 3.34× / 2.28× / 1.85× for 256/512/1024/2048.

### Open, and gating

- **The two legs disagree on chunk size and each is biased toward its own construction.** Leg A's
  judgments are document-level and topical (favours coarse); Leg B anchors a rare entity in one
  deep section and scores by max-rollup (favours fine). **No configuration may be pruned until
  the target query population is declared.** This is a decision, not a measurement.
- **Budget-matched, fine chunks win** — `tok256` beats `tok2048` by 2.2–3.8×, on two corpora,
  two query styles, four breadth rungs. First reading on which the legs agree. Not yet enough
  to settle the size row: one metric family, both constructions favour small chunks, and the
  raw `@1` reading still points the other way.

### Stopped

The **confirmation run** — 90 TREC CDS topics split 10 development / 80 confirmation, six index
arms over one shared 32,663-document corpus, five pre-registered contrasts, and a passage-level
primary endpoint (`EUC@4096`: of the evidence a topic needs, what fraction survives into a
4,096-generator-token context).

**Stage 0**, its development-set calibration, returned `GATE-NOT-EVALUABLE — row 9 precondition
failed` on all five contrasts.

| finding | measured | required |
|---|---|---|
| **A. The gold is not reproducible** — a study stop | span self-consistency **0.323** (0.387 under both alternative readings) | ≥ 0.90 |
| | hallucinated-span rate 0.0504 | ≤ 0.05 |
| | index agreement (verified spans at the claimed position) 0.645 | reported, not gated |
| **B. The endpoint is on a floor** | dev `EUC@4096` **0.017–0.069** | [0.15, 0.90] |
| **C. The binary secondary resolves negatively** | `ES-Hit@4096` discordance **0.10–0.40** | ≤ 0.025 |

The labeling model agrees with itself about *whether* a document contains evidence (0.871) and
disagrees about *where* (0.32). For an endpoint whose entire content is *where*, that is
disqualifying, and no sample size fixes an unstable denominator.

The floor has a mechanism, and it is a mismatch **inside the specification**: §6.3 pools 20
documents per arm per variant, D3 spreads up to 12 units across up to 12 documents, and §7.3 at
B = 4,096 admits 5–10. At that budget the packed context holds **1.1 of a topic's 10.1**
unit-bearing documents. Packing the *entire* frozen D = 50 pool at unlimited budget still leaves
the shipping arm at 0.025 — and at that ceiling `EUC` is nearly proportional to chunk size,
which is the confound the endpoint exists to avoid.

**What has to change before it runs again** (from the measurement, not from taste):

1. Tie the evidence denominator to the budget — either the unit cap follows what a realistic
   context holds, or `EUC` is redefined per *document* rather than per topic.
2. Fix the labeler or the protocol, not the sample size. Quote-primary anchoring (the repair this
   run had to implement anyway) or a reasoning judge. A second judge was provisioned and never
   needed — the first failed against itself.
3. Count budgets and chunk sizes in the same tokenizer, or bound the bias.
4. **ε does not move.** Nothing measured licenses widening the equivalence margin.

### Two things Stage 0 found that outlive it

- **Retrieval, not packing, is the binding ceiling.** Only **28–48%** of evidence-bearing
  documents appear anywhere in the top 50 after reranking. An out-of-pool sample yielded usable
  evidence **75%** of the time against 88.5% for pooled documents — evidence is abundant where
  retrieval does not reach.
- **A cross-tokenizer budget confound.** Counting budgets in the generator's tokenizer and chunk
  sizes in the embedder's under-supplies the coarse arm by **18%** at a "matched" budget, in the
  direction that flatters fine chunks. **I checked the earlier budget-matched runs: `rescore` and
  `breadth-k` count both sides in the same tokenizer** (realised budgets matched to 9% and 13%)
  **and do not carry it.** Their conclusion stands. Any future budget-matched comparison must
  publish realised budgets per arm and name the tokenizer each side was counted in.

## 6. The trap — read this before quoting any Stage 0 number

Applied mechanically, the power rule reads **"passes" on all five contrasts**: σ_d
0.070 / 0.000 / 0.081 / 0.029 / 0.064 against a 0.1577 requirement — the best variance numbers
anywhere in this study. Every one of them is the standard deviation of a quantity pinned at
1.7–6.9% coverage.

**Contrast N2 makes it unmissable.** It returned σ_d = `0.0000` and `p_flip` = `0.0000`: perfect
zero-variance agreement between the shipping configuration and its zero-overlap twin, on all ten
topics. What that means is that both arms covered *identical* evidence — **almost none**. Two
configurations covering nothing is not two configurations shown to be equivalent.

Measured ρ is **−0.046 to +0.064** with every 95% interval containing 0. On its face that is the
good branch. It is uninformative: an intra-class correlation of indicators that flip 0.5–7.3% of
the time has nothing to correlate.

**`GATE-NOT-EVALUABLE` is a third kind of answer** alongside "resolved" and "unresolved": the
instrument failed its own precondition, so its output carries no information about the question —
*including the reassuring-looking output*. The pre-registration caught this only because §7.6
requires the manipulation checks to be read **before** any contrast is. The two checks whose
failure would have meant a plumbing bug both passed exactly (GOLD 1.000 on 10/10, NEGATIVE 0.000
on every arm). The instrument works; it is pointed at something it cannot see.

## 8. Rules that must not be broken

The authoritative lists are [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md) (repo: conventions, hardware, failures and
their fixes, test opt-ins) and the per-user memory index at
`~/.claude/projects/-home-wilke-Development-ragstack/memory/MEMORY.md`. The ones this line of

- **Never stop a service by process-name pattern.** `pkill -f "uvicorn …"` once took down every
  API on the host — a 17-hour outage (#402). Production runs the same command line as every
  scratch server. Stop by the pid recorded at launch, or resolve by port and verify
  `/proc/<pid>/cwd` **and** `cmdline` first. `pgrep` returning nothing is a fleet-wide alarm, not
  proof your cleanup worked.

  production instance; `--qdrant-url ""` resolves right back to it inside the client (#454,

  Prefer no live store at all — point `QDRANT_URL`/`ELASTICSEARCH_URL` at `http://127.0.0.1:1`
  so a leak fails loudly.
- **Pin `PYTHONPATH=<checkout>/python` on every Python invocation.** The editable install in both
  conda envs points at `/rag/repos/ragstack/python`, a legacy production checkout (#432). A
  console script (`uvicorn`) puts its own `bin/` on `sys.path[0]`, not your CWD — that is the
  case that actually bit. **Do not remove the editable install from `/rag/envs/ragstack`**; the
  `:8010` restart recipe in `docs/production-restore.md` is the only way back up and it depends
  on it.
- **GPUs 6 and 7 are reserved.** They read 0 MiB throughout Stage 0 and still do. H100 and A100
