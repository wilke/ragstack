Why RAGStack chunks differently

Same semantic-chunking DNA across the stack — RAGStack adds what an online, multi-tenant retrieval API needs that batch/HPC tools don't.
These are stages of an HPC scientific-RAG pipeline: distllm is the backbone of Argonne's HiPerRAG (3.6M papers; SciQ 90% / PubMedQA 76%; Polaris/Sunspot/Frontier). The real axis is HiPerRAG-style batch corpus-build vs. RAGStack online serving.

The justification  — chunking stage only, not RAG quality

Scope: HiPerRAG's edge is parsing (Oreo) + retrieval encoder (ColTrast) — different stages; this is not a RAG-quality claim. For chunking: RAGStack — the only online-serving system — adds token-exact lossless sizing (auto-detected from the model), real NLP boundaries, and deterministic ids for re-ingest, tenant isolation & citations. A truncated chunk is unsearchable — critical for small-context embedders (SFR-4096: 12% of semantic chunks overflow), less so for long-context (NVEmbed 32k).
